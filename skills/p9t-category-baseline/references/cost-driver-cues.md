# Cost Driver Cues

Prompts by category type to identify the primary cost components and what influences them.

Use when building the cost drivers section of a category baseline. Select the relevant category type and apply the applicable cues. Combine types for hybrid categories (e.g., labour-intensive managed services with technology components).

---

## Labour-intensive categories

*Applies to: FM, cleaning, security, catering, logistics, professional services, contact centres*

| Cost driver | Key questions |
|---|---|
| FTE count | How many people are deployed? How is headcount determined — by volume, site, or SLA? |
| Wage rates | What is the current rate band? Is there a living wage or sector-specific rate floor? |
| Overtime and absence | What is the overtime rate? What is the absence-cover model? |
| Management overhead | What management-to-operative ratio is built in? |
| Shift pattern | Are unsocial hours, weekend rates, or on-call premiums applied? |
| TUPE / workforce transfer | Are there legacy TUPE obligations affecting cost base? |

## Material-intensive categories

*Applies to: packaging, raw materials, industrial consumables, MRO*

| Cost driver | Key questions |
|---|---|
| Commodity index | Is pricing linked to a published index (e.g., steel, oil, aluminium)? |
| Volume leverage | What is the annual volume, and is it consolidated or fragmented across sites? |
| Logistics and distribution | What are the freight costs and lead times? |
| Waste and yield | What is the reject or waste rate, and who bears the cost? |
| Inventory holding | What are the stockholding costs, and who owns the risk? |

## Technology categories

*Applies to: software licensing, SaaS, IT managed services, data*

| Cost driver | Key questions |
|---|---|
| License model | Per-seat, per-use, enterprise, or consumption-based? |
| User count and growth | How many licensed users now, and what is the growth trajectory? |
| Support and maintenance | What is the annual support cost as % of license? |
| Customisation debt | Are there bespoke configurations that lock in the current vendor? |
| Data portability | Can data be extracted cleanly if the supplier changes? |
| Renewal uplift | What is the typical year-on-year renewal price increase? |

## Professional services

*Applies to: consulting, legal, audit, HR advisory, engineering*

| Cost driver | Key questions |
|---|---|
| Rate card structure | How are rates set — by grade, project type, or framework? |
| Utilisation | What percentage of booked time is billed? What is the on-site vs. remote split? |
| Scope creep | What change control mechanism governs additional work? |
| Travel and expenses | What is the T&E policy and how is it charged? |
| Knowledge transfer | What is the plan at end of engagement — is knowledge retained internally? |

## Infrastructure / capital-intensive categories

*Applies to: construction, plant and equipment, utilities, facilities fit-out*

| Cost driver | Key questions |
|---|---|
| Capital vs. opex split | Is this a capex purchase, lease, or service fee? |
| Lifecycle cost | What are the maintenance, upgrade, and end-of-life costs? |
| Energy consumption | What is the energy intensity and how is it priced? |
| Regulatory compliance | Are there inspection, certification, or retrofit obligations? |
| Mobilisation cost | What are the upfront costs and how are they amortised? |

---

## Cross-category cues (always check)

- **Indexation**: Is pricing subject to inflation indexation (CPI, RPI, sector-specific)? Who bears the risk?
- **Volume dependency**: Does unit cost change materially with volume? Is there a cliff-edge pricing structure?
- **Currency exposure**: Is any portion of cost in a foreign currency? Who holds the FX risk?
- **Regulatory change risk**: Are there upcoming regulatory changes (minimum wage, environmental standards, safety requirements) that will affect the cost base?
