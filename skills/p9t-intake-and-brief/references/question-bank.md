# Question Bank

Clarification questions for sourcing intake, organised by dimension.

Load when the user's request is ambiguous or incomplete. Select only the questions relevant to the current gap — do not present the full bank to the user at once.

---

## Objective clarity

- What is the underlying business need driving this request?
- What does success look like in 12 months?
- Is this a new need or a continuation of something already in place?
- What is the cost of inaction — what happens if nothing changes?

## Scope boundary

- Which sites, business units, or geographies are in scope?
- Are there adjacent categories that should be included or explicitly excluded?
- Is this a single-lot or multi-lot sourcing event?
- Are there services currently bundled that should be separated, or vice versa?
- Is there a pilot or phased rollout expected?

## Constraint discovery

- Are there approved supplier lists, sustainability requirements, or policy constraints?
- Are there legal or regulatory requirements the category must comply with?
- Is there a budget ceiling, and has it been formally approved?
- Are there preferred commercial structures (fixed price, open book, gain share)?
- Are there incumbent contract terms (notice periods, IP, data) that limit options?

## Stakeholder mapping

- Who is the business owner accountable for this sourcing event?
- Who has authority to approve scope before work begins (G1)?
- Who must sign off before award (G4)?
- Are there stakeholders with strong incumbent preferences we should know about?
- Who from legal and finance needs to be engaged, and when?

## Timeline realism

- What is the hard deadline — contract expiry, go-live, compliance date?
- Is this timeline driven by a business event or a preference?
- Has the timeline been agreed with the approval authority?
- Are there internal review cycles (legal, finance, board) that must be factored in?
- What happens if the timeline slips — is there a contingency?

## Incumbent context

- Is there a current supplier? Contract expiry date?
- What is the current performance level — any known issues?
- Is the intent to retender openly or pursue a renewal negotiation?
- Are there any known risks with the incumbent (financial, performance, relationship)?
- Does the incumbent have proprietary systems or data that create switching risk?

## Risk and sensitivity

- Are there aspects of this category that are politically sensitive internally?
- Is the supply market known to be concentrated or fragmented?
- Are there regulatory, safety, or reputational risks in this category?
- What is the impact if the sourcing event fails to produce a result?
