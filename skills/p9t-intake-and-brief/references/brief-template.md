# Brief Template

Structured template for a sourcing brief.

---

## 1. Objective

What the organisation is trying to achieve. Not "buy X" — the underlying business need.

- State the business driver: cost, risk, capacity, compliance, continuity, or capability
- Distinguish the immediate need from the strategic goal
- One to two sentences. If it takes a paragraph, the brief is not ready.

## 2. Scope

| In scope | Out of scope |
|---|---|
| [Service / product / geography / BU] | [Adjacent categories, future phases, excluded locations] |

- State explicitly what is excluded. Ambiguous scope creates rework downstream.
- Confirm whether this is single-lot or multi-lot.

## 3. Requirements

**Nice-to-haves** — evaluated but not blocking:

**Constraints** — policy, legal, technical, geographic:
